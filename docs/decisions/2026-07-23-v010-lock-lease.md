# v0.10 lock, lease, and ownership decision

## Date

2026-07-23

## Question

Does v0.10 evidence justify adding a proposal lock, lease lifecycle, enforced
owner field, or INDEX-level serialization?

## Evidence boundary

The controlled observation period was
`2026-07-23T13:46:38.485722+00:00` through
`2026-07-23T13:46:39.817578+00:00`. It contains two anonymous logical
operators, three sessions, two proposals, and six snapshot-controlled
lifecycle mutation attempts. It is hermetic opt-in evidence, not production
telemetry or a human adoption sample.

The release drill runner also executes seven dedicated fault scenarios for
archive/INDEX failure, partial metadata writes, stale caller context,
proposal rename/external move, and future schema rejection.

The complete aggregate is
`evals/team-trials/v010-controlled-trial-v1.json`. Recovery details are
executable through `scripts/run-recovery-drills`.

## Gate

A lock or lease proposal requires at least one of:

1. reproducible authoritative-state loss caused by overlapping mutation;
2. an observed same-proposal conflict rate that snapshot refresh and the
   one-owner operating rule cannot manage;
3. repeated derived-state corruption that `validate-index` and
   `rebuild-index` cannot repair; or
4. a demonstrated need for cross-machine long-lived ownership with defined
   stale-owner recovery.

Presentation-only ownership requires repeated handoff ambiguity plus a
versioned extension design covering authority, approval projection, unknown
version behavior, and migration. A label alone is not a lock.

## Decision

**NO-GO** for lock, lease, enforced ownership, and INDEX-level CAS in v0.10.
Keep cooperative concurrency:

- one active operator per proposal;
- different short names and separate Git worktrees for parallel work;
- fresh `status` after handoff;
- snapshot CAS for stale caller context; and
- archive authority plus explicit INDEX validation/rebuild.

The threshold was not met. The one observed INDEX incident was derived-only
and recovered 1/1 without moving or rewriting authoritative archive records.
The three deliberate stale-context attempts were rejected, but that drill
does not claim safe overlapping writes or make snapshot CAS a lock.

Any lock, lease, or ownership extension is out of scope for this proposal and
requires a new SDD proposal with its own failure model, schema, migration,
recovery, and rollback tests.

## Re-evaluation trigger

Reopen the decision only when a new opt-in aggregate and reproducible fixture
meet a gate above. Do not infer need from a hypothetical race, an isolated
rebuildable INDEX, or an unreviewed transcript.

## Rollback boundary

This decision and playbook wording add no artifact field or runtime state.
They can be revised without schema migration. If future evidence meets the
gate, preserve the current cooperative path until a separately approved
mechanism passes its activation and rollback tests.
